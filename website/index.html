Anything predictable becomes wallpaper. The chime you expect is the chime you stop hearing. Silent Bell never lets you expect it — each tap lands at a random moment, so it stays a surprise, and a surprise is the one thing attention can't ignore.

HOW IT WORKS
i
Start it
Open the app, tap Start. Choose how many taps an hour feels right — one to ten. Then put your wrist down.
ii
It finds you
Taps land at random moments. You can't brace for one, so you can't tune it out. Each one is a small return.
iii
Only you know
No sound. No banner. Nothing on the watch face. To everyone around you, nothing happened.
iv
Through everything
Do Not Disturb, Sleep, Work Focus — the tap arrives anyway. It's yours, not a notification.

AN HONEST NOTE

Apple pauses background sessions after an hour. So Silent Bell pauses too.

When it does, your watch gives one last pattern you'll recognise, and a note appears: Silent Bell paused. Tap Resume to start a new session. One touch starts the next hour.

We could dress this up as a feature. Honestly, it's a platform limit — but it makes a fair bell of its own. Once an hour, you notice the noticing stopped, and you choose to begin again.
