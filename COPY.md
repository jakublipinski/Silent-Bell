# App Store copy

Every string that appears on the App Store product page, in all five languages
the app ships. This is the source of truth: App Store Connect is where it is
entered, but this is where it is written and reviewed.

Character limits are hard caps enforced by App Store Connect. Counts below were
measured, not estimated.

| Field | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | 30 | Highest ASO weight |
| Subtitle | 30 | Second highest |
| Keywords | 100 | Comma-separated, no spaces between items |
| Promotional text | 170 | Editable without review |
| Description | 4000 | Barely indexed; does the selling |

**Never repeat a word between name, subtitle and keywords.** Apple indexes each
term once, so repetition spends the budget twice for one result.

## Category

**Primary: Health & Fitness. Secondary: Productivity.** Health &
Fitness is where the mindfulness audience browses, including Apple's own
Mindfulness app. Productivity is the honest second home for the attention
framing.

## A note on the translations

German, Japanese, French and Spanish were machine-translated (by Claude) and
have **not been reviewed by a native speaker**. The app is sold largely on the
quality of its writing, so this is the weakest part of the listing. Anything
here should be treated as a first draft that a human translator improves, not
as finished copy. The in-app strings carry the same caveat.

The word *Silent Bell* is a brand and stays untranslated everywhere, including
inside sentences.

## Why the description says what it says

The **AN HONEST NOTE** section is load-bearing and must survive every edit. The
hourly pause is the single most predictable one-star review this app can
attract. Stated up front it reads as honesty; discovered cold it reads as a
defect. Do not let it be trimmed for being off-message.

The closing line about public source code does double duty: it substantiates the
privacy claim, which is otherwise unverifiable in a compiled binary, and it
justifies the price to a reader who wonders why a small app costs anything.
